    Which buildings are worth a solar retrofit?

    Pick a pilot building or search an address. The model builds the building and its neighbours from OpenStreetMap, simulates a typical year hour by hour, and compares BIPV cladding, flexible modules, roof racking and solar louvres on every surface.

    VPV is a research direction of SPFI, the open solar research platform for Israel. Its method, assumptions and sources are published with every result.
