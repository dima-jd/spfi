The kibbutz vote

A new procedure for kibbutzim and moshavim sends land deals to the general assembly before signing. For farm solar, the members’ meeting becomes part of the project.

Select an underlined term for a short explanation.

Much of Israel’s AgriPV potential sits on land farmed by kibbutzim and moshavim. These communities are Cooperative societythe legal form of most kibbutzim and moshavim, supervised by the Registrar., and their dealings are overseen by the Registrar of Cooperative Societies. In September 2026 the Registrar published a new procedure on how a cooperative chooses partners, negotiates and signs contracts. It replaces a 2021 version.

The procedure does not mention solar. It matters to solar because of how it defines a Material transactiona deal that must be approved by the members’ general assembly.. Any deal concerning real estate, other than a residential lease, qualifies. So does any deal outside routine business, any deal with an office holder, and any deal above a value threshold each cooperative sets for itself. A long lease or option for an AgriPV array, a storage yard or a ground-mounted plant fits that description. A law firm, Erdinast Ben Nathan Toledano, reached the same reading in a client summary cited by the radar.

Material transactions must be approved by the general assembly before signing. The draft agreement has to be attached to the meeting’s agenda so members can read it first. Members with a conflict of interest must declare it, leave the room and stay out of the vote, and the cooperative’s legal adviser must give a written opinion. Splitting one deal into smaller ones to avoid the assembly is forbidden. The board must also choose counterparties through a uniform written invitation and record its reasons.

For developers, this adds a visible step that cannot be negotiated away in a back office. For members, it creates a moment to ask the questions that last for 20 or 25 years. Which crops will continue under the panels? Who carries the loss if the new AgriPV rules cut the tariff? What happens to the land at the end of the lease?

A vote is only as good as the information in front of it. The procedure guarantees that members see the contract. It does not guarantee they see an agricultural plan, a landscape drawing or a risk analysis. SPFI will watch whether developers start bringing those to the assembly too.
