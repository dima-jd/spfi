Methods

How we build a story

We select reporting, research and announcements, check the available original sources, and write original English and Hebrew explanations. Discovery links are leads, not evidence. Sources appear beside the article so readers can examine the underlying claims.

What the evidence supports

Company claims, laboratory findings, projections and editorial interpretation are attributed and distinguished. A proposal is not a completed installation; a completed installation does not prove a particular outcome. Missing information stays visible.

How to read the map

The current map separates reviewed projects, official planning records, a sample of 500 agricultural parcels, industrial authority aggregates and public-area modelled potential. It is a dated snapshot, not a live operating feed. Planning status does not establish construction. Screening does not establish technical suitability, land rights or grid access.

Dates and editorial placement

Source publication dates, observation dates and record reviews describe different events. Trending and promoted selections are chosen by SPFI; they are not readership rankings or paid sponsorship. Urgent posts are editorial priorities, not breaking-news alerts.
