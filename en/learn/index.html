How does a solar panel make electricity?

Photovoltaic cells convert incoming light into electrical energy. An inverter converts the panel’s direct current into alternating current for compatible electrical systems. Solar water heating is a different use of the sun.

US Department of Energy · Solar photovoltaic technology
What makes agrivoltaics different?

Agrivoltaics combines agricultural activity with solar electricity on the same land. Panel layout, crop needs, access for machinery and the division of responsibilities all matter. Crop yield or water savings should be demonstrated for the actual site and crop.

MIGAL · Agrivoltaics Center
Does a map marker mean a project is operating?

No. A planning record, a potential site and a reviewed project describe different things. AGRIPV keeps them separate. Planning approval alone does not prove construction, grid connection or electricity production.

Where can I begin evaluating a roof?

Israel’s Ministry of Energy provides a solar-potential map. Treat modeled potential as a starting point. An actual project still requires site-specific checks of the structure, shading, connection and operating arrangements.
