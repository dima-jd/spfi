Shade as public infrastructure

Israel is paying towns to roof their sports courts with solar panels. The first test is whether the result is a good public space, not only a power plant.

Select an underlined term for a short explanation.

An open asphalt court in July is close to unusable at midday. In July 2025 the government approved a national programme to put Solar canopya roof structure, open at the sides, that carries panels and shades the space beneath. over public sports fields, with NIS 34 million for 2025 and an estimated 3,500 fields nationwide. The pitch combines four things: shade, rain cover, longer playing hours and electricity that earns the council money.

In July 2026 the Energy Ministry named 42 local authorities for the next round of grants. Reported figures are NIS 15 million in total, NIS 300,000–450,000 per authority depending on its Socio-economic clusterIsrael’s 1–10 ranking of local authorities, used here to size grants., more than 25,000 m² of canopy and over 3 MW of panels. Or Yehuda, for example, received NIS 375,000. A grant is a commitment, not a court: councils still need a site, a design, a building permit, a contractor and a grid connection.

Tel Aviv offers the clearest built example. In August 2025 the city’s Economic Development Authority and Apollo Power hung lightweight, flexible solar film on a tensioned cable net over the court at Hatzri school in Tel Baruch. The city judged it on safety, appearance, cost, output and full shade. On 21 October 2026 Apollo’s subsidiary SolarPaint reported an order worth about NIS 3.8 million to roof more Tel Aviv courts, for completion in 2027 subject to permits and grid approval. A ministry event report in November said about 60 municipal staff toured the pilot.

Financing is getting easier too. Israel’s Knesset has exempted PV structures that cover an area or a building from certain drainage, road and sidewalk levies until the end of 2030.

What is still missing is evidence about the space under the panels. None of the public sources SPFI checked report the pilot’s capacity, its output or how much cooler the court is. A canopy can be a generic steel shed or a piece of civic architecture with good light, drainage, acoustics and night lighting. Councils choose which one they get when they write the tender.
