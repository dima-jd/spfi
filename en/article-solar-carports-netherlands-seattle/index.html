Why a canopy starts two goals down

Solar canopies cost more than roofs and produce less per square metre. The Dutch government’s answer is to build them big and wire them to nearby industry.

Select an underlined term for a short explanation.

Israel is betting on solar canopies over sports fields, parking lots and cycle paths (see “Shade as public infrastructure” and “One tender, many surfaces”). The Netherlands has been through the same enthusiasm and has published a blunt assessment of the economics.

The cost problem. A market study by the consultancy Arcadis, commissioned by the Dutch climate ministry, found that Solar carporta canopy over parking spaces that carries solar panels. are much more expensive than rooftop or field systems. The main reason is the steel or timber structure that a roof provides for free. Carports also generate less per square metre, because parking layouts use space less efficiently. One Arcadis adviser summed it up: a carport is “two goals down before you begin”. Given a choice, a bank will usually finance a roof.

The Dutch answer. The Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO), in guidance checked in June 2026, names grid congestion, negative electricity prices and tighter financing as the main obstacles. It recommends focusing on large public parking canopies of at least MWpmegawatt-peak, the rated output of panels under standard test conditions., ideally connected to the electricity demand of a nearby industrial estate, and points to an investment subsidy and a state guarantee fund as possible support. Arcadis also argued for backing self-consumption and On-site PPAa contract to sell electricity directly to a consumer next to the generator, without using the public grid., so the power is used next door rather than exported into a congested grid.

A small canopy, slowly. The University of Washington in Seattle shows the time scale. Its pilot canopy over parking lot E18 covers about 30 spaces, carries roughly 86 kW of panels and 20 EV chargers, and was celebrated as complete in February 2026. The university issued its environmental determination for the project in January 2024: two years for one pilot. Even the capacity varies between sources (84, 86.4 and 93 kW), a reminder to check numbers against the owner’s final record. UW describes the pilot as a test for covering the whole lot and the neighbouring one.

For Israeli councils, the lessons are concrete. Pair canopies with a local load: a sports centre’s lights, a school, a nearby factory. Bundle sites to reach a scale lenders like. And count the canopy’s civic value, its shade and rain cover, as part of the case, because on electricity alone it will rarely beat a roof.
