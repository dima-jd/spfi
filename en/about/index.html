About SPFI

Curious about possibilities. Careful with claims.

SPFI is an independent educational publication. We select useful stories, read the original reporting or research, and write our own English and Hebrew explanations. Telegram helps us discover stories; it is not proof of a claim.

Research findings, company claims and editorial interpretation are distinguished. Historical reports keep their dates. Mentioning an organization does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

What you will find here

  • Stories and explainers on solar energy in Israel and around the world, in six sections: Agrivoltaics, Research & technology, Industry & policy, People & communities, Environment, and Storage & grids.
  • The AgriPV Israel map ↗: reviewed agrivoltaic projects, official planning records, candidate farm fields and modelled solar potential, each with its source and date.
  • Solar in numbers: capacity, electricity generation and national targets in context.

How we work

Every article lists its sources beside the text, so readers can check the underlying claims. Read our methods

Data and permissions

The AgriPV map uses public data from the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security, the Planning Administration and the Survey of Israel / GovMap, under written terms from each agency. Official data is shown as published, and SPFI's own analysis is labelled as such. The GovMap solar-potential layers are for non-commercial use.

Independence

SPFI currently publishes no paid content, ranks no installers and sells no equipment. Press-release briefings are attributed to their issuers.
